<p class="news-lead">Four days of field measurements. Five research projects. A shared ambition: to turn valuable data into better detection capabilities.</p>

<p>From 6 to 9 October 2026, the <strong>4D Perception Lab (4DPL)</strong> conducted a major measurement campaign at <strong>DOVO in Meerdaal, Belgium</strong>. Working alongside our partners and the DOVO teams, we collected a substantial dataset to support research into the detection of explosives and their traces, camouflaged objects, and landmines.</p>

<h2>From field data to detection algorithms</h2>

<p>The campaign brought our research into the field, with measurement activities spanning outdoor scenes, camouflage setups and close-range observations. Together, these measurements provide valuable material for advancing detection methods across several complementary research areas.</p>

<p>The next step is to put this dataset to work. The collected data will contribute to the <strong>development and training of advanced detection algorithms</strong>, supporting 4DPL’s ongoing work in hyperspectral imaging, perception and AI for defence research and mine action.</p>
